Building a cleaner circular economy.

OUR MISSION

Make responsible recycling effortless.

Ecolyy aims to make recyclable waste easier to sort, schedule, collect and move into the recycling ecosystem.

By connecting users with organized collection operations, Ecolyy helps turn everyday recycling into a simple habit.

A circular approach

Materials that still have value should have an opportunity to return to the circular economy instead of becoming unmanaged waste.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Simple systems create better habits.

Simple
