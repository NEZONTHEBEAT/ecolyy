THE PROBLEM

India’s Recycling System Is Broken.

Recyclable materials often end up mixed with general waste because convenient, organized collection is still difficult.

Unsorted Waste

Valuable recyclable materials often get mixed with everyday garbage.

Waste Accumulation

Recyclables pile up at homes and become difficult to store.

Unclear Disposal

People often don't know where or how recyclable materials should go.

Irregular Collection

Collection can be unpredictable, inconvenient and unorganized.

HOW IT WORKS

From waste to reward.

A simple six-step journey designed to make responsible recycling effortless.

Sort Your Waste

Select the recyclable categories you want to hand over.

Schedule Pickup

Choose your preferred date, time and address.

Partner Assigned

A verified ReKart partner receives your request.

Waste Collected

Your partner arrives and collects the recyclable material.

Weight Verified

The actual collected quantity is recorded and verified.

Get Your Reward

Your reward is calculated based on the verified collection.
